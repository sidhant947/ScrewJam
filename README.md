# ScrewJam

A clean, satisfying, and privacy-respecting screw puzzle game. Unscrew plates, match colors with active toolboxes, and clear complex multi-layered mechanisms.

---

## Features

- **100% Privacy First**: Zero tracking, zero analytics, zero data collection. Your data never leaves your device.
- **No Ads**: Completely distraction-free gameplay. No banners, no popups, no rewarded ads.
- **100% Offline**: Play anywhere, anytime without an internet connection. All assets and fonts are bundled locally.
- **Random Puzzle Mode**: Generate fresh, standalone puzzles across 5 distinct difficulty tiers (Easy, Medium, Hard, Expert, Master).
- **Pinch-to-Zoom and Pan**: Inspect intricate multi-layer assemblies with fluid pinch-to-zoom gestures and double-tap to reset.
