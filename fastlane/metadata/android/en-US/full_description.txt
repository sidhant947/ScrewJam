Screw Jam is a clean, satisfying, and privacy-respecting screw puzzle game. Unscrew plates, match screw colors with active toolboxes, and solve intricate multi-layered mechanisms.

Screw Jam respects your privacy:
- Zero tracking, zero analytics, zero data collection.
- Completely ad-free. No banners, popups, or rewarded ads.
- 100 percent offline. Play anywhere without an internet connection.
